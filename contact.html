Frequently asked

Common questions about engaging an engineering consultant

Do I need a structural engineer for a small renovation or extension?+

Most councils and building surveyors require structural engineering input wherever works affect load-bearing elements — this often includes alterations, extensions, retaining walls and even some internal renovations. We can advise early whether your project needs structural input or certification.

What's the difference between a building surveyor and a structural engineer?+

A building surveyor or certifier assesses overall compliance with the National Construction Code and issues permits. A structural engineer designs and certifies the structural elements themselves. Most projects need both, working together.

Can Lingenious manage my project as well as provide the engineering?+

Yes. Engineering and project management or construction advisory can be engaged together or separately, depending on how much day-to-day coordination your project needs.

Do you provide fire engineering reports and performance solutions?+

Fire safety engineering services are delivered by, or coordinated with, appropriately qualified and registered fire safety practitioners as part of the Lingenious project team.

What areas do you service?+

We are based in Melbourne and work primarily across Victoria, with additional experience across transport, rail and civil infrastructure projects. Get in touch with your project location and we'll advise whether we're well placed to help.

How long does a structural assessment or defect investigation take?+

Turnaround depends on scope and site access. We aim to acknowledge every enquiry within one business day and will confirm a realistic programme once we understand the site and requirements.

Do you only work on new projects, or can you help with existing building issues?+

Both. We regularly assess existing buildings and infrastructure for defects, structural adequacy and remediation options, alongside new design and delivery work.

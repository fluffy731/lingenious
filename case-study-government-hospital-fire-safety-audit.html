Hospital Fire Safety Compliance

Fire Risk Audit & Assessment — Major hospital campuses, Melbourne

Fire Safety

This case study reflects the professional experience of our fire safety engineering lead, delivered prior to and independently of Lingenious Consulting.

Statutory periodic fire safety audits
Fire risk assessments across live hospital campuses
Coordinated with hospital operations & health authority requirements
01 · Challenge

Auditing live clinical facilities

Hospital campuses must undergo periodic statutory fire safety audits and risk assessments, which need to be carried out without disrupting live clinical operations or patient care.

02 · Approach

Working around operational and patient safety constraints

Our fire safety engineering lead carried out statutory fire safety audits and fire risk assessments across several major hospital campuses, working directly with hospital facilities teams to schedule inspections around operational and patient safety constraints.

03 · Outcome

Compliance sustained across critical care facilities

Each campus's fire safety audit was completed and reported to the standard required by the relevant health authority, supporting continued compliance for these critical care facilities.

Relevant experience of our leadership team, delivered prior to and independently of Lingenious Consulting, is noted where applicable.

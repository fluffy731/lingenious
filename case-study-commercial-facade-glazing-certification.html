Multi-Building Development

Glazing Systems Certification — Residential development, New South Wales

Facade
5 glazing product types verified
3 buildings assessed
AS/NZS 1170.2 & AS 2047 compliance
01 · Challenge

Proving standard products would meet project-specific wind loads

A façade contractor needed to demonstrate that standard aluminium window and door systems — already independently tested by the manufacturer — would meet the specific wind and water-penetration requirements of this three-building development, without the cost and delay of commissioning new physical testing.

02 · Approach

Engineering verification against independent test data

Lingenious engineered a structural adequacy check comparing the manufacturer’s independent test data against the project’s site-specific wind loads and water penetration requirements under AS/NZS 1170.2 and AS 2047, across fixed windows, hinged doors, awning windows and sliding door configurations.

03 · Outcome

Certified compliant without additional testing

All five glazing systems were certified as structurally adequate for the project, allowing procurement to proceed on the existing product range without additional testing cost or delay.

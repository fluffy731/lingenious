Commercial Office Tower Refurbishment

Fire Safety Engineering — Commercial office towers, Melbourne CBD

Fire Safety

This case study reflects the professional experience of our fire safety engineering lead, delivered prior to and independently of Lingenious Consulting.

Fire Engineering Reports & Performance Solutions
Multiple CBD office tower upgrades
Coordinated with base-building compliance
01 · Challenge

Upgrading occupied towers without disproportionate cost

Refurbishment and upgrade works across several occupied CBD office towers needed to satisfy current fire safety requirements without disrupting base-building operations or triggering disproportionate upgrades to unrelated parts of each building.

02 · Approach

Scoping fire safety to the actual works

Our fire safety engineering lead prepared fire engineering reports and performance-based solutions for each refurbishment, working through the existing base-building fire systems and egress arrangements to scope only the upgrade work genuinely required by each project.

03 · Outcome

Proportionate strategies, no unnecessary disruption

Each refurbishment proceeded with a fire safety strategy proportionate to the actual scope of works, avoiding unnecessary disruption to the operating buildings.

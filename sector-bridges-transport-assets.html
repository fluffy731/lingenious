Bridges + Transport Assets

Bridge and transport asset inspection, condition assessment and structural advisory for councils, road and rail authorities and infrastructure asset managers.

Who this is for

Councils, road and rail authorities and infrastructure managers responsible for the safety, condition and lifecycle management of bridges and transport assets.

Common challenges

  • Meeting regulatory inspection frequencies, including Level 1 and Level 2 inspections.
  • Managing ageing bridge and transport asset stock with constrained renewal budgets.
  • Assessing load rating and access constraints for current and future use.
  • Prioritising capital works across a broader asset portfolio.
  • Coordinating inspections around live traffic, rail operations or public access.

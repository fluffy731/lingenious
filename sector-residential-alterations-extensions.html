Residential Alterations + Extensions

Structural and civil engineering for house alterations, extensions, second-storey additions and renovations that affect load-bearing elements, footings or site levels.

Who this is for

Homeowners, builders, architects and building designers working on house extensions, alterations, second-storey additions and renovations where structural, footing or retaining works are involved.

Common challenges

  • Existing structure, footings or as-built drawings are undocumented or unclear.
  • Councils and building surveyors require structural sign-off before a permit can issue.
  • Retaining walls, site levels and drainage need to be resolved alongside the building works.
  • Balancing structural compliance with construction budget and buildability.
  • Coordinating structural design with the architect's or builder's staging and programme.

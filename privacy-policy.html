Privacy Policy

How Lingenious Consulting collects, uses and stores information submitted through this website.

This page describes, in plain terms, the data this website actually collects and where it goes. It is intended as a factual summary rather than formal legal advice — Lingenious Consulting should have this reviewed against the Australian Privacy Principles and its own record-keeping practices before relying on it as a complete privacy policy.

Information collected through enquiry forms

When you submit the Fast Enquiry or Detailed Project Brief form on the Contact page, we collect the information you enter — which may include your name, email address, phone number, company, project address, project stage, timeframe, required services, a project description, and any file you choose to upload.

How enquiry information is used

Information submitted through the enquiry forms is used only to respond to your enquiry, assess whether Lingenious is well placed to assist, and, where relevant, to prepare a proposal or scope of work. It is not sold, rented or used for unrelated marketing.

Third-party services used by this website

  • Formspree — enquiry form submissions are processed and delivered to us by Formspree, a third-party form-handling service. Formspree receives the same information you enter into the form, including any uploaded file.
  • Google Fonts — this site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which may result in a request to Google's servers when a page loads.
  • Google Maps — the map on the Contact page is an embedded Google Maps frame, subject to Google's own privacy practices.
  • Analytics — at the time of writing, no analytics or advertising tracking is active on this site. If an analytics service (such as Google Analytics) is added in future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Document uploads

Files uploaded through the Detailed Project Brief form (up to 10 MB, in the formats listed on the form) are transmitted via Formspree and used solely to help us understand and respond to your enquiry.

Contacting us about your information

To ask what information we hold about you, or to request that it be corrected or deleted, email solomon@lingenious.com.au.

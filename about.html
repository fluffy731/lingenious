About Lingenious Consulting

Engineering judgement backed by project delivery experience.

Lingenious Consulting is an engineering-led professional services firm based in Melbourne. We provide multidisciplinary engineering, project management and construction advisory services across residential, commercial, government and infrastructure projects.

Our approach is built around practical technical judgement — considering not only whether a solution can be engineered, but whether it can be approved, coordinated, constructed, maintained and delivered within the realities of the project.

  • Understand the actual problem

    We begin by defining the project constraints, risks and required outcomes rather than applying a predetermined solution.

  • Coordinate the interfaces

    Civil, structural, façade, ground, fire and construction requirements often overlap. We identify and manage those interfaces early.

  • Provide practical advice

    Our recommendations consider compliance, constructability, cost, programme, safety and asset performance.

  • Remain involved

    Where required, we support clients beyond design through procurement, construction, defects, completion and handover.

  • Responsive Communication

    We acknowledge project correspondence and design queries within one business day. If further assessment is needed, we'll confirm a clear response timeframe.

  • Value Management

    We design for constructability and cost efficiency from the outset. Structural systems, footings and drainage are optimised to reduce cost and complexity without compromising safety or compliance.

Founder · Managing Director + Principal Engineer

Dr Solomon

Chartered Civil and Structural Engineer

Solomon is a Chartered Civil and Structural Engineer with experience across buildings, transport infrastructure, structural assessment, asset management and multidisciplinary project delivery.

Holding a PhD in Civil Engineering from Monash University, he leads the technical direction and project advisory functions of Lingenious. His experience spans civil and structural engineering, façade and building assessment, infrastructure inspection, construction-stage advisory, defect management, asset handover and major project coordination.

His approach combines engineering rigour with practical consideration of constructability, compliance, commercial constraints and long-term asset performance.

Beyond client work, Solomon contributes to the profession as an Engineers Australia interview panelist for Chartered and National Engineering Register (NER) assessment in Structural Engineering, a committee member for Standards Australia's AS 3600 (Concrete Structures), a guest lecturer for Monash University's Master of Advanced Engineering program, and an educator with Western Sydney University and TAFE NSW, where he helped devise and now delivers a multidimensional BIM course.

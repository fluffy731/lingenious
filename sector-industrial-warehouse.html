Industrial + Warehouse Developments

Civil, structural and geotechnical engineering for industrial, warehouse and logistics facilities, including hardstand and yard infrastructure.

Who this is for

Developers, owners and operators of industrial, warehouse and logistics facilities, and their architects and builders, delivering new facilities or hardstand and yard infrastructure.

Common challenges

  • Heavy floor and pavement loading needs to be designed for current and future operations.
  • Large-span structural systems bring different design and constructability considerations.
  • Large impervious areas require careful site drainage and stormwater design.
  • Staged construction and authority approvals need to be sequenced with the build programme.
  • Civil, structural and geotechnical scope needs to be coordinated across the one site.

Councils + Public Infrastructure

Civil, structural and asset advisory support for local councils, government agencies and their contractors responsible for public buildings and municipal infrastructure.

Who this is for

Local councils, government agencies and their delivery contractors responsible for community buildings, sporting and recreation facilities, and municipal civil and structural infrastructure.

Common challenges

  • Ageing asset stock needs to be prioritised against limited capital works budgets.
  • Public safety and risk exposure must be actively managed across a portfolio of sites.
  • Procurement, probity and reporting requirements shape how engineering advice is delivered.
  • Civil and structural scope often needs to be coordinated across multiple facilities at once.
  • Findings need to be reported clearly to council engineers and asset managers.

Relevant Lingenious services

Civil Engineering Structural Engineering Construction + Asset Advisory

Typical deliverables

  • Asset condition assessments and inspection reports
  • Civil and structural design for facility upgrades
  • Defect and remediation prioritisation across multiple sites
  • Council and authority coordination support

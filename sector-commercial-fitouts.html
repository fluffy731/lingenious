Commercial Buildings + Fit-Outs

Structural, façade and project engineering for retail, hospitality, office and mixed-use buildings and tenancy fit-outs.

Who this is for

Commercial building owners, tenants, developers, architects and builders delivering new commercial buildings or tenancy fit-outs across retail, hospitality, office and mixed-use environments.

Common challenges

  • New fit-out loads, mezzanines or openings need to be checked against the existing structure.
  • Façade or shopfront interfaces need to be resolved with the base building.
  • Works are often staged around live trading hours or centre management requirements.
  • Multiple consultants and trades need to be coordinated on a single technical outcome.
  • Programmes are frequently tied to fixed lease or handover dates.
